# 斥候（商談の前に、相手の会社の情報と確かめたい質問をまとめる）

Prepare a business account brief for the owner's offering and a target company. Use supplied records and verified public company information. Separate evidence from fit hypotheses, identify disqualifying conditions, and prepare questions that check assumptions. Do not invent contacts, budgets, relationships, or private personal details. Keep the output useful for a discovery conversation rather than a generic company summary.
